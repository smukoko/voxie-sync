Set up Voxie

Read a book on your Kobo, keep going as an audiobook on your phone, and come back to the page where the audio stopped. Setup itself takes about ten minutes. Making the accounts it uses takes longer, so the next page lets you do that first.

Have these ready

  • Your Kobo and its USB cable.
  • Three small accounts (a server, a narration GPU and a voice director). The next page walks you through them, and each one is optional.
  • A short clip of the narrator's voice, if you want the best voices. You can also narrate for free on this Mac.

Make your accounts first

These take about 20 minutes in all, and every later step goes faster when they already exist. Setup has not started: skip any of them now and Voxie asks for it when it is needed.

  1. Hetzner the server, about €5 a month

    Make an account and add a payment method. Hetzner sometimes checks a new account before it lets you rent a server, which can take a while: start with this one. Already run a Voxie server? Skip it.

    Open Hetzner Cloud

  2. RunPod the narration GPU, under $1 a novel

    Make an account, add a few dollars of credit, then Settings, API Keys, Create API Key and keep the key somewhere you can copy it. Skip it to narrate for free on this Mac, slower and with preset voices.

    Open RunPod

  3. OpenRouter the voice director, about $0.10 a novel

    Make an account, add a few dollars of credit, then Keys, Create Key and keep the key too. Without it the narrator reads without direction.

    Open OpenRouter

  4. A voice clip 8 to 15 seconds

    Your own recording, or a YouTube link to someone who agreed to be cloned, with the start and end times and the exact words said.
